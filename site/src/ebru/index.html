Ebru

Turkish paper marbling, simulated. Drops of paint spread on the water and push each other aside, then a stylus and combs draw the pattern. Every drop, stroke and colour comes from the seed.

Patterns

Palettes

Traditional pigment sets. “Auto” lets the seed choose.

Battal: sprinkled drops only. Gel-git: a stylus drawn back and forth, then across. Taraklı: gel-git finished with a fine comb. Bülbül yuvası (“nightingale’s nest”): taraklı with small spirals. Hatip: concentric drops pulled into hearts over a battal ground.
