Seeded sky

A sky at any hour with any amount of cloud, from a seed string. The air is simulated: Rayleigh and Mie scattering, ozone and the earth's shadow give the blues, sunsets and twilight. Clouds are real volumes ray-marched through 3D noise, lit by the sun with multiple scattering and fading into the haze with distance. Same seed, same sky.
