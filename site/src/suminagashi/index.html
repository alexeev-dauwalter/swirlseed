Suminagashi

“Floating ink”, the Japanese marbling. Brushes touch the water in turn, one with sumi ink and one with a clear surfactant, so the ink spreads in thin rings. A breath over the tray bends them, and a sheet of washi lifts the print. The seed decides where every brush touches.
